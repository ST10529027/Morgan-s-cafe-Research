MORGAN’S CAFÉ – ABOUT US RESEARCH

Business background

Morgan’s Café is a fictional café created for this website project. The café is based in Gauteng and has two branches, one in Pretoria CBD and another in Centurion. The café gives customers a comfortable place to enjoy food and drinks, meet with friends, or take a break from their day.

Mission

Morgan’s Café aims to provide good quality food and drinks while making sure customers receive friendly service in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Vision

Morgan’s Café aims to become a popular local café where customers can enjoy good food and drinks while spending time with friends, family or colleagues.

Target audience

The main target audience includes students, young professionals, families, local residents and people looking for a comfortable place to meet, work or relax.

Brand message

The tagline for Morgan’s Café is:

“Made for Good Moments.”

Content note

Morgan’s Café is a fictional organisation, so the business background, mission, vision and target audience have been created as original content for this website project.
