MORGAN'S CAFÉ – PRODUCTS RESEARCH

Research purpose

This research was done to prepare content for the Products page of the Morgan's Café website. The menu will give customers a clear view of the food and drinks available, together with their prices.

Content note

Morgan's Café is a fictional organisation created for this website project. The menu items, descriptions and prices will be original content created for the website. Online café menus may be used to compare realistic South African pricing, but their content will not be copied.
